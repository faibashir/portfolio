How I work

A reviewable design process for AI training, engineering review, and real hardware work.

1

Define requirements

Clarify electrical constraints, interfaces, operating ranges, signal requirements, and expected deliverables.

2

Model and simulate

Use SPICE analysis to validate operating points, frequency behavior, transients, and tolerance-sensitive assumptions.

3

Capture schematic

Build clean schematics with labels, test nodes, references, notes, and clear separation of analog, digital, and power blocks.

4

Layout and review

Route the PCB, run ERC/DRC, review manufacturability, export fabrication files, and document design decisions.
